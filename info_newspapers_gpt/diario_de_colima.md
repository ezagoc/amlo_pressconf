## Notes

SIC lists Editora Diario de Colima, S.A. de C.V. as the publishing house and gives address/circulation details. A 2024 Diario de Colima note says Gati, S.A. de C.V. is the controlling company of Diario de Colima and announced Mario Alavez as the new director. Because this identifies director/control company but not an individual owner, `owner.person` is blank.
