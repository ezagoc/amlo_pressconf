## Notes

The official "Quiénes somos" page says Hidrocálido is a daily publication edited in Aguascalientes by Empresa Editorial de Aguascalientes, S.A. de C.V., founded in 1981. It lists Agustín Morales Padilla as founder and José Luis Morales Peña as director general. The privacy notice gives the legal entity and address. The site has a "Periódico en PDF" area, supporting print/digital classification.

The outlet describes itself as independent and without links to business, political, or religious groups. I therefore coded ideology as `center` and 4T alignment as `unclear`, with low confidence rather than an ideological claim.
