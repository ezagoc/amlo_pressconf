## Notes

The Estado de México directory lists Carmen Lira Saade as directora general, Carlos Payán Velver as director fundador, and the Toluca address. La Jornada's main legal notice identifies Demos, Desarrollo de Medios, S.A. de C.V. as holder of La Jornada internet rights. Media Ownership Monitor describes La Jornada as employee/cooperative structured through Demos and historically left-oriented. I code left with medium confidence, but keep 4T as `unclear` for the regional edition.
