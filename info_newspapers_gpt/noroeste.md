## Notes

Noroeste is a Sinaloa regional newspaper founded in Culiacan on September 8, 1973. Its code of ethics says it was founded as an independent newspaper by the Sinaloa businessmen Manuel Clouthier, Enrique Murillo and Jorge del Rincon, with Silvino Silva as founding editorial director, and with the objective of forming citizens to have better governments in Sinaloa.

The current directory lists Adrian Lopez Ortiz as Director General, Guillermina Garcia Nevares as Directora Editorial, Elizabeth Peraza as Directora Comercial, Elva Aguirre as Subdirectora Corporativa de Administracion, Hector Castro as Editor of Noroeste.com, and Manuel Serrano as Coordinador Comercial Digital.

The directory gives two office/entity addresses. For Culiacan it lists Editorial Noroeste S.A. de C.V. at Angel Flores 282 oriente, Colonia Centro, Culiacan, Sinaloa, CP 80000. For Mazatlan it lists Sistema Periodistico de Sinaloa S.A. de C.V. at Rio Amazonas 602-A, Campo Bello, Mazatlan, Sinaloa, CP 82010. The privacy notice gives Editorial Noroeste S.A. de C.V. and an address on Avenida Benemerito de las Americas No. 708, Campo Bello, Mazatlan, CP 82010. Recommended action: store both Culiacan and Mazatlan addresses, and mark the exact current legal domicile as needing reconciliation if address precision matters.

Platform coding should be `print + digital + social media + video`. A 2024 article describes the transition from printed newspaper to digital platforms, social networks, and Noticieros Noroeste. A 2025 article quotes Adrian Lopez Ortiz saying Noroeste is not defined by paper but by the information it publishes in new digital/audiovisual formats.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `independent / civic-democratic / watchdog; historically anti-authoritarian and anti-government-control`.

Do not code simply as PAN, `aligned`, or `opp`. The founding history includes Manuel Clouthier del Rincon, later a major PAN figure, and the Clouthier family remains part of the newspaper's history. However, Noroeste repeatedly presents its institutional line as independent, plural and civic-democratic rather than partisan. Its code of ethics says the outlet is committed to truthful, objective, independent and plural journalism, independent economically and editorially from private, government or group interests, and says journalists should not actively militate in a party in a way that compromises Noroeste's independence.

The strongest historical/ideology evidence is that Noroeste was founded against a context of PRI-era/government-controlled press, with a mission of citizen formation, free press and better government. Anniversary pieces and interviews describe it as a free/independent newspaper founded because Sinaloa needed a newspaper able to investigate and publish without asking government permission. A 2023 Manuel Clouthier Carrillo anniversary column also describes periods of government reprisals/boicots against Noroeste under Sinaloa governors.

Thus, for an ideology field, the best label is not left/right. Better labels are `independent`, `watchdog`, `civic-democratic`, `anti-authoritarian`, or `anti-government-control`, with a separate note: `founder Manuel Clouthier del Rincon later became a leading PAN figure; avoid equating institutional line with PAN alignment without period-specific evidence`.
