## Notes

La Razon de Mexico is a national newspaper founded on May 18, 2009. The workbook already lists the legal/comsoc entity as `L.R.H.G. INFORMATIVO, S.A. DE C.V.`, which matches the official privacy notice, official directory footer, SIC record and Media Ownership Monitor.

The current official directory lists Mario Navarrete as Director General, Adrian Castillo de los Cobos as Director Editorial, Ileana Salinas as Directora Ejecutiva, Gilda Cruz Terrazas as Subdirectora de Edicion, and Julian Mendez as Gerente Administrativo. The directory footer names Adrian Castillo de los Cobos as editor responsible and gives the address at Plaza Galerias / Circuito Interior 193, Torre Privanza piso 7, Colonia Veronica Anzures, Miguel Hidalgo, CP 11300.

The official privacy notice identifies L.R.H.G. Informativo, S.A. de C.V. as responsible for personal data, with domicile at Melchor Ocampo 193, Colonia Veronica Anzures, Miguel Hidalgo, CDMX, CP 11300. SIC gives the same broad address, lists the casa editorial as L.R.H.G. Informativo, S.A. de C.V., and lists Monday-Saturday periodicity.

Ownership/person-level note: Media Ownership Monitor identifies Ramiro Garza Cantu as the individual owner and founder of La Razon de Mexico, and links him to Grupo R in the oil/energy sector. MOM also identifies Ramiro Garza Vega as president of the board and son of Jose Ramiro Garza Cantu. Because this MOM profile is 2018/2019-era, code Ramiro Garza Cantu as `owner_person_source = MOM` and mark current ownership as needing verification if exact current control matters.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `center-right / business-oriented / critical of AMLO-4T`.

Do not code as a formal party outlet. The strongest evidence is indirect but meaningful:

- Media Ownership Monitor identifies ownership through Ramiro Garza Cantu, founder of Grupo R in oil/energy services. This supports a business-sector ownership context.
- The opinion pages and current article/tag patterns include repeated critical framing of AMLO, Morena and the 4T. Examples include opinion pieces titled "AMLO y el giro a la izquierda", "Morena y la izquierda", "La ola feminista, la oportunidad perdida de AMLO", "Critica, pluralidad y disenso", "Tambores de guerra?", "AMLO, anti-arqueologo" and "Con AMLO no se transformo nada". These are column/opinion pieces and should be treated as editorial-line evidence, not hard ownership facts.
- I did not find a strong neutral external source that labels La Razon's ideology as PRI, PAN, Morena, left or right. Therefore a cautious label is preferable: `business-oriented / center-right / critical of AMLO-4T`, with confidence medium.
