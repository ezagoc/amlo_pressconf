## Notes

The official Aguas legal notice states that the site is published under license from Empresa Editorial de Aguascalientes, S.A. de C.V., owner of the print and online editions. The privacy notice gives the same company and the Quinta Avenida 101 address. A media-rate listing describes Aguas as a daily morning supplement of Hidrocálido, founded January 3, 2000, published by Empresa Editorial de Aguascalientes, S.A. de C.V., with 25,000 copies inserted daily in Hidrocálido.

No reliable current individual owner evidence was found in this pass. Ideology is coded `center` / `unclear` because I found no strong evidence of partisan alignment.
