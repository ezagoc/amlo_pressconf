## Notes
Imagen del Golfo is the digital/statewide brand of Corporativo Imagen del Golfo. Its directory lists Jose Pablo Robles Martinez as editor and director general and Monica Robles de Hillman as president of the board. The privacy policy names Imagen del Golfo Multimedios, S.A. de C.V. as the responsible entity for imagendelgolfo.mx, imagendeveracruz.mx and diariodelistmo.com. Article 19's Veracruz de los Silencios identifies the broader La Voz del Istmo / Imagen del Golfo group as controlled by the Robles Barajas and Hillman family. Ideology remains `center / unclear / low`.
