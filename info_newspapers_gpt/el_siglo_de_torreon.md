## Notes

El Siglo de Torreon is a regional daily newspaper based in Torreon, Coahuila, serving the Comarca Lagunera. Its official anniversary/history article says Antonio de Juambelz y Bracho founded the paper on February 28, 1922. The paper presents itself with the slogan "Defensor de la comunidad" and describes its origin around the rise of Torreon and the need for a regional newspaper for the Laguna region.

The current official directory lists Francisco Juaristi Santos as director general, Javier Garza Ramos as director editorial, and Pablo Salazar Velazquez as director de informacion. The privacy notice identifies Cia. Editora de la Laguna, S.A. de C.V. as responsible for personal data and gives the Torreon address. AEE lists the outlet as part of Grupo Celsa / Cia. Editora de la Laguna, with certified circulation figures for 2024.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `regional civic / independent by self-description; current national alignment unclear`.

Do not code as `aligned` or `opp` from the evidence currently found. The strongest direct source is the outlet's own editorial policy page, which says its information is independent and free of influences from government, private, religious or partisan groups. That is a self-description, not external validation, but it is still the clearest source for the current editorial identity.

For historical/political context, the paper has long presented itself as a local/regional civic institution through the slogan "Defensor de la comunidad." I did not find a strong, sourceable current partisan alignment comparable to the Grupo Canton cases or to the historical ideology evidence for Diario de Yucatan.
