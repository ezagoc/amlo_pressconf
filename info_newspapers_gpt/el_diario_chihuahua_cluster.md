## Notes

The El Diario group is linked through Osvaldo Rodríguez Borunda and the expansion of El Diario from Juárez into Chihuahua, Nuevo Casas Grandes, Delicias and other editions. The outlet's own history emphasizes independence, plural political access, and critical reporting, but I did not find a current, stable pro/anti 4T label. I therefore use `center` and `unclear`.
