## Notes
El Debate's own pages say the paper was founded in Los Mochis in 1941 by Manuel Moreno Rivas and later acquired/directed by the Salido family. Its principles page explicitly says El Debate is private property of the Familia Salido Artola and names Luis Javier Salido Artola as executive president of Empresas El Debate. The outlet self-describes as independent and states it does not receive government, party, or state financing. I would code `center / unclear / low` because I found no reliable external left-right label.
