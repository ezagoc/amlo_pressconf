## Notes

The official El Imparcial Baja California directory pages list Juan F. Healy Loera as president of the board, Ricardo Salido Gaxiola as executive director in Baja California, Lourdes C. Lugo as editorial director, and the Tijuana/Mexicali addresses. The footer identifies the site as part of Grupo Healy and gives "Impresora y Editorial S.A. de C.V." as copyright holder. I did not find a strong current partisan label, so ideology remains `center` / `unclear`.
