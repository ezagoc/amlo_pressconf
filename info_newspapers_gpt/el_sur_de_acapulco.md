## Notes

The official directory lists Juan Angulo Osorio as director general. A 30th anniversary/conversatorio item describes El Sur as a Guerrero reference point and an example of journalism financed from society, highlighting coverage of social struggles and human-rights issues. A profile/anniversary text says Juan Angulo Osorio, Tomás Tenorio Galindo and Maribel Hernández created El Sur and published the first issue on May 3, 1993. I code ideology as left with medium confidence because the available evidence points to social-movement/human-rights orientation, but 4T alignment remains `unclear`.
