## Notes

The strongest current legal-source evidence I found is the Diario de México edictos/privacy material, which identifies Editorial DDM, S.A. de C.V. as the responsible entity for Diario de México, gives the Chimalpopoca 38 address, and says Diario de México is a registered mark of Editorial DDM, S.A. de C.V.

There is older/parallel institutional evidence for Editoriales de México, S.A. de C.V. The SIC record for Diario de México lists Editoriales de México, S.A. de C.V. as the casa editorial and Federico Bracamontes Galvez as director. This matches Diario de Morelos' own history, which says Federico Bracamontes Gálvez was director general of Editoriales de México when Diario de Morelos was launched.

Federico Bracamontes Gálvez is the founder figure. Diario de México anniversary material says the newspaper began on March 2, 1949 and was founded by Federico Bracamontes Gálvez. Diario de Morelos biographical material says he created Diario de México, Diario de México USA, Diario de Morelos, La Extra in Los Angeles, Mix FM / La 99, and La Comadre. It also describes his wider public role in journalism and the creation of Día del Abogado.

For current person fields, I would be cautious. MPM lists Federico Bracamontes Baz as director general of Diario de México in 2026 and Omar Salvador González Zárate as editorial director. A USPTO mirror record for the "DIARIO DE MEXICO" mark lists Federico Gustavo Bracamontes Baz and Miguel Ángel Bracamontes Baz among the trademark parties/applicants. This is strong evidence of Bracamontes family control/involvement in the brand, but I would not treat it alone as a clean owner-person field unless the workbook accepts trademark-party evidence as ownership evidence.

Diario de México is historically related to Diario de Morelos. A Diario de Morelos column calls Diario de México the "hermano mayor," says it was directed by Federico Bracamontes Baz, and says Diario de México, Diario de Morelos, La Comadre, and La 99 formed Grupo Bracamontes under Miguel Bracamontes Baz. Code this as same family/media-group network, not as one newspaper owning the other.

For ideology, I found no reliable source supporting a current partisan or 4T-alignment label. Suggested coding remains `center` / `unclear` with low confidence.

## Relationship to Diario de Morelos

Recommended wording: historically related through Federico Bracamontes Gálvez, Editoriales de México, Grupo Braca / Grupo Bracamontes, and the Bracamontes Baz family.

Do not code: "Diario de México owns Diario de Morelos" or "Diario de Morelos owns Diario de México."

Better coding:

- `owner.family`: Familia Bracamontes / Bracamontes Baz
- `owner_group`: Grupo Diario de México / Grupo Bracamontes
- relationship note: Historically tied to Diario de Morelos / Grupo Braca through Federico Bracamontes Gálvez and the Bracamontes family.

## Caveats

- `legal_name`: use Editorial DDM, S.A. de C.V. for current web/privacy/trademark evidence; note that SIC and older institutional listings use Editoriales de México, S.A. de C.V.
- `owner.person`: leave blank unless you want to code Federico Bracamontes Baz as current visible director/brand-control figure rather than strict owner.
- `year_founded`: 1949 is well supported; the specific date is March 2, 1949.
