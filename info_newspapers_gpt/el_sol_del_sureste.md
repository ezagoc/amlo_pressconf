## Notes
MPM lists El Sol del Sureste as founded August 13, 2001 and edited by Sistema Informativo de Tabasco. Its own anniversary articles identify it as part of SIT with Diario Presente and Diario Avance, and say Ignacio Cobo Gonzalez is the director general of SIT. Mary Fe Diaz Izquierdo is described as founding director of the paper. SIC has an older/alternate casa editorial, Calles e Hijos, S.A. de C.V.; I used current MPM/SIT evidence for the workbook. Ideology stays `center / unclear / low`.
