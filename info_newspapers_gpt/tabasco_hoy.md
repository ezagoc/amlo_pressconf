## Notes

Tabasco Hoy is the founding/anchor outlet of Grupo Canton. A Grupo Canton biography says Tabasco HOY began on December 1, 1987. The Sistema de Informacion Cultural lists it as a daily newspaper under Organizacion Editorial Acuario, S.A. de C.V., with Miguel Canton Zetina as director and an address in Centro, Tabasco.

The outlet should be coded as part of the same Grupo Canton cluster as Diario Basta, Quintana Roo Hoy, and Campeche Hoy. For ideology/alignment, the strongest coding is `aligned / pro-4T`, with a source note rather than treating it as a purely factual attribute.
