## Evidence and notes

Noticias de Queretaro appears in media directories under the short title `Noticias`, but the listed web domain and address identify it as the Queretaro daily at `noticiasdequeretaro.com.mx`. MPM lists it as a morning daily, founded October 27, 1971, with Aida Ivonne Garfias Torres as president and general director, and gives the Ezequiel Montes address. SIC lists the casa editorial as Editora Offset Color, S.A. de C.V., with daily periodicity and the same general address/phone set. Mediavyasa also lists the outlet as `Noticias de Queretaro`, print advertising rates, the Ezequiel Montes address, and the web domain.

For ownership, the clearest evidence I found is from Tribuna de Queretaro. It says Editora Offset Color S.A. de C.V. publishes Noticias de Queretaro and reports company shareholding inside the Garfias family: Rogelio Garfias Ruiz as founder and majority shareholder at the time described, with Aida Ivonne Garfias Torres and other family members also listed as shareholders. Since Rogelio Garfias Ruiz died in 2011, I would treat Aida Ivonne Garfias Torres as the current visible leadership figure, not necessarily as sole owner unless a current corporate registry confirms it.

For ideology, I would code cautiously as `right / unclear / low`. I did not find a formal self-description or independent scholarly label assigning the outlet an ideology. The basis is indirect: recent social/social-syndicated posts give prominent favorable institutional coverage to Queretaro's PAN state/local officials, especially Mauricio Kuri, Felifer Macias, Luis Nava, and Agustin Dorantes; Libertad de Palabra criticized the paper for privileging official information after a Morena-related incident; and Tribuna de Queretaro documented local government advertising to Noticias. This supports a local government/PAN-establishment tendency, but it is not enough for a high-confidence ideological coding. For `ideology.4t`, use `unclear` rather than `opp` unless more direct editorial evidence is found.
