## Evidence and notes

Pulso Diario de San Luis is a San Luis Potosi daily founded on August 30, 1988. The paper's own anniversary pieces identify Don Miguel Roman Valladares Garcia as founder and describe Pulso as the flagship project that later generated other media products, including San Luis Hoy, Canal 7 XHSLV/XHSLS, Huasteca Hoy, Pulso Online, and later SinEmbargo.mx. The official directory lists Miguel Valladares Garcia as president of the Consejo de Administracion, Pablo Valladares Garcia as director general, Luis Fernando Fernandez Montiel as subdirector general, and Elma Guadalupe Torres Mendez as gerente administrativa.

For the legal entity, SIC lists the casa editorial as `Editora Mival, S. A. de C. V.`. MPM lists Pulso as published by `Editora Mival, S.A.` with RFC EMI-870826-V84. Pulso's own directory states `Diario Matutino publicado por EDITORIAL MIVAL, S.A. de C.V.` and also identifies `Editora Mival, S. A. de C.V.` as distributor at Galeana No. 485. I would standardize the workbook value as `Editora Mival, S.A. de C.V.` and note the official-site spelling variant.

For ownership/control, use the Valladares family with caution. Current official directory evidence identifies Miguel Valladares Garcia as president of the board and Pablo Valladares Garcia as director general. Pulso's anniversary/history pieces describe the newspaper as founded by Miguel Roman Valladares Garcia and continued by Miguel Fernando and Pablo Valladares Garcia. This is stronger evidence of family control than of exact shareholding.

For ideology, I would code `center / unclear / low`. I did not find a reliable external ideological label. Pulso's own founding/anniversary pieces repeatedly describe its line as plural, honest, objective, independent, open to all currents of thought, and "sin injurias." It has also highlighted investigations into corruption and defended freedom of expression, including against local government initiatives. Recent opinion columns sharply criticize the Gallardo/PVEM state government and discuss tensions with Morena/Claudia Sheinbaum circles, but that shows watchdog/opposition-to-local-power behavior more than a stable national left-right ideology. For `ideology.4t`, use `unclear`: the outlet is critical of some actors linked to the local/regional 4T coalition, but I did not find enough evidence to mark it simply as `opp`.
