## Evidence summary

Public radio network of the Sonora state government. XHHB-FM began operations on 8 October 1982 and now feeds a statewide repeater network.

## Ideology rationale

Center avoids assigning a permanent partisan ideology to a public broadcaster. Aligned records its current institutional control by the Sonora government and public communication system under a Morena administration, not a claim about every program.

## Verification note

The 4T label is structural and can change with state administrations. Current management, the distinction between the 1982 launch and 1985 OPD decree, and the legal status of each repeater should be checked.
