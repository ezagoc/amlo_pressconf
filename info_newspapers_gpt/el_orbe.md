## Notes

The Secretaría de Cultura SIC listing identifies El Orbe as a daily newspaper in Tapachula, gives the address, lists Editora Zamora Cruz, S.A. de C.V. as the publishing house, and names Enrique Zamora Cruz as director. A La Jornada report from 2005 describes Enrique Zamora Cruz as director general and owner. A 2026 digital edition front page names Don Juan Zamora Velázquez as founder and shows current print presence/social accounts.

I left year founded blank because the sources found are not perfectly consistent: the 2026 edition says "Año XLVII", while older event text says El Orbe reached 33 years in 2010. Ideology remains `center` / `unclear` with low confidence.
