## Notes

The official Diario de Morelos legal notice says the outlet was founded in Cuernavaca on April 17, 1978 by Federico Bracamontes Gálvez, has its headquarters in Cuernavaca, and is a local Morelos edition. The same page identifies Miguel Ángel Bracamontes Baz as Presidente Ejecutivo de Grupo Diario de Morelos and lists the directory as: Editoriales de Morelos, S.A. de C.V. as publisher; Federico Bracamontes Gálvez as founder; Miguel Ángel Bracamontes Baz as president; Eduardo Lavín Cedillo as vice president; Oyuki Zapata Torres as vice president for administration/finance; and Asaid Soto Álvarez as technology director.

The privacy notice identifies Editoriales de Morelos, S.A. de C.V. as the personal-data responsible entity and repeats the Av. Morelos Sur 132 address. The SIC record gives the same house/publisher, associates the outlet with Grupo Braca, and lists Miguel Ángel Bracamontes Baz as director.

Diario de Morelos is historically tied to Diario de México through Federico Bracamontes Gálvez and the Bracamontes family. The official "Quiénes somos" page says Federico Bracamontes Gálvez was director general of Editoriales de México when he launched Diario de Morelos. A Diario de Morelos column says Diario de México was the "hermano mayor" of Diario de Morelos and that Diario de México, Diario de Morelos, La Comadre in Cuautla, and La 99 in Cuernavaca formed Grupo Bracamontes under the presidency of Miguel Bracamontes Baz. Treat this as a family/media-group relationship, not as proof that Diario de México currently owns Diario de Morelos.

For platform, the official "Quiénes somos" page and anniversary material describe a multiplatform operation: print, radio, television, web, and social media. The homepage links to free printed versions / e-paper, mobile apps, and digital/social channels.

For ideology, I found no strong external source supporting a left/right or 4T-alignment label. The official legal/editorial policies emphasize independence, separation of advertising and editorial content, fact-checking, and balanced electoral coverage. Suggested coding is therefore `center` / `unclear` with low confidence.

## Relationship to Diario de México

Recommended wording: historically related through Federico Bracamontes Gálvez, the Bracamontes Baz family, Editoriales de México, and Grupo Braca / Grupo Bracamontes.

Do not code: "Diario de México owns Diario de Morelos."

Better coding:

- `owner.family`: Familia Bracamontes / Bracamontes Baz
- `owner_group`: Grupo Diario de Morelos / Grupo Braca
- relationship note: Historically tied to Diario de México / Editoriales de México through Federico Bracamontes Gálvez and the Bracamontes family.
