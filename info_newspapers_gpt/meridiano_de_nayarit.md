## Notes

The strongest corporate source found is Grupo Alica's Meridiano company page. It lists Meridiano as one of Grupo Alica's companies, gives the legal name as Compania Editorial Alpesor, S. de R.L., describes the business line as communications/journalism, identifies Jorge Enrique Gonzalez Castillo as gerente general, and gives the Tepic address at Av. Independencia 335, Fracc. Las Aves.

MPM lists Meridiano de Nayarit as a daily morning newspaper in Tepic, founded in 1942, with Jorge Enrique Gonzalez Castillo as director and the same Av. Independencia 335 address. This supports coding it as print + digital. The Meridiano site also has an "Edicion Impresa" section and footer/contact material with Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter and YouTube links. Its Linktree lists website, printed-edition download, Spotify podcast, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok and Threads.

I would not code Jorge Enrique Gonzalez Castillo as `owner.person`. The evidence supports director/gerente general. A 2022 article by Gonzalez on Meridiano discusses a shareholder conflict in Compania Editorial Alpesor: he says shareholders representing 67 percent of shares named him director general and manager, while 33 percent remained with the Alfaro succession. This is useful ownership-context evidence, but too messy for a clean individual owner field.

For `year_founded`, MPM says 1942. However, Hemeroteca Digital de Nayarit lists Meridiano de Nayarit circulation from July 30, 1986 to the present. Use 1942 only if accepting the broader El Nayar-Meridiano lineage; otherwise leave year founded blank or document the 1986 current-title caveat.

For ideology, I found no reliable source supporting a left/right or 4T-alignment label. Suggested coding is `center` / `unclear` with low confidence.
