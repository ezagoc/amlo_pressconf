## Evidence summary

Sonora state television company that began transmissions on 30 May 1959. Official transparency pages identify Televisora de Hermosillo, S.A. de C.V., its current director and Hermosillo address.

## Ideology rationale

Center avoids treating a state broadcaster as permanently left or right. Aligned reflects current ownership and coordination by the Sonora government under a Morena administration, not uniform support in every program.

## Verification note

The alignment label is institutional and administration-dependent. The original founders, detailed state shareholding and exact division between commercial and public concessions require confirmation.
