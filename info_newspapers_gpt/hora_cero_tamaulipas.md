## Notes
Hora Cero's own "Nosotros" page says it first circulated in Reynosa in February 1998 and led to Verbo Libre Editores. It names Heriberto Deandar Robinson as director general. The legal/privacy pages identify Grupo Verbo Libre Editores and Editora Hora Cero, S.A. de C.V.; SIC also lists Editora Hora Cero, S.A. de C.V. and the Reynosa address.
