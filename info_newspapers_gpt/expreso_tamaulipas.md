## Notes
Expreso's anniversary article says the paper first circulated February 27, 1995 in Ciudad Victoria and was founded/led by Pedro Alfonso Garcia Hernandez, Francisco Cuellar Cardona and Victor Contreras. The site uses the group label Grupo Editorial Expreso-La Razon. I left legal name blank because I did not find a clean current corporate imprint. Ideology is `center / unclear / low`: Expreso has documented critical/watchdog journalism and one of its reporters, Antonio de la Cruz, was described by La Jornada as critical of the Tamaulipas government, but that does not map cleanly to national left-right or 4T alignment.
