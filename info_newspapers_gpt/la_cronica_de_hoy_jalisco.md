## Notes

La Cronica de Hoy Jalisco appears in the workbook as a separate Jalisco outlet with the older domain `cronicajalisco.com`. Current live Jalisco coverage is now available as a section of the national site at `https://www.cronica.com.mx/jalisco/`, and the official national directory includes a Jalisco staff block. That supports treating the Jalisco entry as either a regional edition/section of La Cronica de Hoy or as a local edition under the same group, depending the project's row-level needs.

The national directory lists Francisco Armenta Solis as Jalisco coordination and Ricardo Gomez Nunez, Ignacio Perez Vega, Lino Gonzalez Corona and Samantha Ivana Lamas Ramirez as Jalisco reporters.

For founding year, LinkedIn lists La Cronica de Hoy Jalisco as founded in 2013, with headquarters in Guadalajara and address at Av. Vallarta 3233, Galerias Exhimoda. Regional media literature also lists La Cronica de Hoy Jalisco as a generalist print/electronic newspaper edited by Grupo Editorial Cronica, with 2013 as founding year and a small print run. Use this as useful but secondary evidence.

The old domain `cronicajalisco.com` appears stale. A web status/rank page crawled in 2026 reported it offline and noted an expired SSL certificate. The national `cronica.com.mx/jalisco/` section is active in 2026, with recent Jalisco articles by the same names in the directory.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `group-level PRI-adjacent / anti-left / anti-AMLO historically; Jalisco-specific alignment uncertain`.

For ideology, avoid over-reading the Jalisco edition by itself. The strongest evidence applies to the national Cronica group: Media Ownership Monitor characterizes La Cronica de Hoy as historically close to PRI and opposed to the left/AMLO, and Courrier International describes the national paper as close to PRI and oriented to Mexico City's economic/social elite.

Jalisco-specific evidence is weaker. Current Jalisco coverage includes reporting on state and local governments, including Jalisco/Guadalajara authorities. I found examples that are favorable or descriptive toward local policy actors, but not enough to assign a stable local-party alignment. If the workbook has only one ideology field, use the group-level label with a note: `Jalisco edition; ideology inferred from Cronica group, not independently verified`.

## Workbook Note

The workbook currently lists `LA CRONICA DIARIA, S.A. DE C.V.` for both La Cronica de Hoy and La Cronica de Hoy Jalisco, which is consistent with group/legal evidence. The URL for Jalisco should likely be updated from `https://www.cronicajalisco.com/` to `https://www.cronica.com.mx/jalisco/` or stored as `old_url`/`current_url`.
