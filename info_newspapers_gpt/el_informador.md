## Notes

The official privacy and terms pages identify Unión Editorialista, S.A. de C.V. and Guadalajara addresses. El Informador's historical pieces state that the newspaper was founded on October 5, 1917 by Jesús Álvarez del Castillo, remained under the Álvarez del Castillo family, and has had four generations of leadership. A 2023 recognition article says Juan Carlos Álvarez del Castillo received the recognition as director. Ideology is coded `center` / `unclear`; the outlet's self-description emphasizes positive/constructive/orienting journalism rather than partisan alignment.
