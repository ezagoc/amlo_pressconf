## Notes

Diario Basta is part of Grupo Canton. Its own anniversary article says it was founded in Mexico City on August 6, 2010, originally with a more popular/entertainment-oriented profile and later transformed into a general-information and political outlet. The same article explicitly links the outlet's transformation to the Cuarta Transformacion / AMLO period.

The Sistema de Informacion Cultural lists Diario Basta under Editorial Prosperidad, S.A. de C.V., with Miguel Canton Zetina as director and a Miguel Hidalgo, CDMX address.

For ideology/alignment, code as an interpretive variable rather than a factual one. Suggested basis: Grupo Canton self-description around the 4T, AMLO-era mañanera participation, and external reporting alleging favorable coverage/publicity contracts.
