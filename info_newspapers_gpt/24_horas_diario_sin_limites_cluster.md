## National Notes

The national 24 Horas site and directory identify the brand as a daily newspaper edited by Informacion Integral 24/7, S.A.P.I. de C.V. The current directory lists Armando Estrop Almaguer as Director General and Alberto Gonzalez as Director Editorial, with Alberto Gonzalez Martinez as editor responsible. It gives the publication address as Ejercito Nacional No. 216, Piso 13, Colonia Anzures, Miguel Hidalgo, CP 11590, CDMX.

The official privacy notice also names Informacion Integral 24/7, S.A.P.I. de C.V. and gives the same Ejercito Nacional address. Dun & Bradstreet lists the company as doing business as "24-HORAS EL DIARIO SIN LIMITES" and lists Eduardo Jose Fernandez Garcia as a key principal/board member; treat this as corporate-directory evidence, not necessarily editorial ownership.

The Google Play app listing says 24 Horas was founded on October 13, 2011, and describes it as a Mexican newspaper whose print edition is distributed free in Mexico Monday-Friday, with WhatsApp distribution to 28 countries. Anniversary columns on 24 Horas also identify October 13, 2011 as the launch date and refer to Antonio Torrado Monge as founder / president of the council or board. Because these are columns/anniversary texts, use them as historical notes; keep the legal entity as the primary ownership field.

## Regional Edition Notes

### Puebla

24 Horas Puebla has a complicated trajectory. A 2020 farewell/cycle-closing article says it was born on September 23, 2015 under Mario Alberto Mejia, circulated daily for four years and ten months, and closed its print cycle on July 31, 2020 due to a multifactor crisis tied to the pandemic and advertising-contract cancellations. A 2024 "Refundacion" column says the Puebla edition returned to the national group, ending the franchise model, and announced print publication beginning January 8, 2024. The current Puebla directory lists Luis Fernando Soto Beltran as editor in chief and names Paola Aroche Flores and Gabriela Arellano Romano for social/web.

### Quintana Roo

The Quintana Roo directory gives strong outlet-level metadata: editor responsible Cesar Munoz Padilla, address at Plaza Mexico Local 309 in Cancun, reserve-of-rights number, licitud certificate, and phone 998 734 7027. A videos page describes it as a leader in print and digital with presence on YouTube, Facebook, Twitter and Instagram.

### Campeche

The Campeche site is active and has "version impresa" and local reporting, but I did not find a clean current official directory/privacy page for editor/address in this pass. Use national legal metadata and mark local address/editor as missing.

### Yucatan

The Yucatan directory gives strong outlet-level metadata: editor responsible Alberto Gonzalez Martinez, address in Altabrisa, Merida, reserve-of-rights number, licitud certificate and phone 999 406 0298. The contact page lists Facebook, X, Instagram, Issuu, TikTok and YouTube accounts.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `political-news / institutional / business-oriented; current partisan alignment unclear`.

Do not code as simply `aligned`, `opp`, pro-AMLO or anti-AMLO from the evidence found. The strongest direct evidence about editorial identity is the Google Play/app description and anniversary material: the outlet is a free general-information newspaper with a political profile and an editorial practice/code of practices. One anniversary article describes it as focused on information that affects decision-making processes and notes distribution in political/business/government settings.

There are opinion columns critical of the 4T/AMLO and also standard institutional/government coverage, but I did not find a strong neutral source giving a party or ideological label. If the workbook needs a single ideology value, use `unclear` or `institutional/business-oriented`; if you need an AMLO-period stance, use `critical of 4T in opinion pages; not enough for a full outlet label`.
