## Notes

La Cronica de Hoy is a national daily newspaper founded on June 17, 1996. The official "Sobre nosotros" page says the newspaper's only editorial line is Mexico and describes the outlet as committed to truthful, professional information and democratic citizenship.

The official directory lists Jorge Kahwagi Gastine as president, Fernando Maron Kahwagi and Jorge Kahwagi Macari as vice presidents, Rafael Garcia Garza as director general, Arturo Ramos Ortiz as subdirector, and Rodrigo Pacheco Peral for web. However, Jorge Kahwagi Gastine died on March 23, 2026. Because the directory was still listing him after his death, preserve this as a succession caveat rather than a clean current-president field.

The Sistema de Informacion Cultural lists La Cronica de Hoy at Londres 38, Colonia Juarez, CP 06600, Cuauhtemoc, CDMX, with La Cronica Diaria, S.A. de C.V. as casa editorial, daily periodicity, and 42,394 copies. Media Ownership Monitor also lists La Cronica Diaria, S.A. de C.V. and the same Londres 38 address.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `PRI-adjacent / anti-left / anti-AMLO historically; self-described opposition to what harms Mexico`.

This is one of the clearer cases where an external media-ownership source provides an ideology/political-line characterization. Media Ownership Monitor says La Cronica de Hoy has been criticized since its beginnings as close to the PRI and openly opposed to the left, especially Andres Manuel Lopez Obrador. Courrier International similarly describes the paper as serious, addressed to Mexico City's economic/social elite, and close to the PRI.

The owner's/president's own language also supports an opposition/watchdog self-description: in a 2022 Cronica interview, Jorge Kahwagi Gastine said he was inspired by having an opposition newspaper and that Cronica would always be opposition to what harms Mexico. That should be treated as self-description, not as neutral external validation.

For the workbook, `opp` is defensible if it means opposition to AMLO/left governments. A more precise label is `PRI-adjacent / anti-left / anti-AMLO historically`, with a note that the outlet self-describes its line as national interest / "Primero Mexico."

## Jalisco Edition Note

The official directory has a Jalisco staff section, and the current live Jalisco coverage is on `https://www.cronica.com.mx/jalisco/`. The older workbook URL `https://www.cronicajalisco.com/` appears stale/offline based on web status checks, and the Jalisco edition should probably be normalized as a section/regional edition of the Cronica group rather than a fully separate outlet unless your project needs it as a separate row.
