## Notes

Proceso is a national Mexican weekly magazine and digital news outlet founded in 1976 after the rupture at Excelsior. The common origin story is the July 8, 1976 "Golpe a Excelsior," when Julio Scherer Garcia and allied journalists left or were forced out after conflict with the Luis Echeverria government and internal cooperative power struggles. Proceso emerged later that year as a weekly magazine dedicated to investigation and political analysis.

Media Ownership Monitor identifies Comunicacion e Informacion, S.A. de C.V. as the company that edits the weekly Revista Proceso and operates Proceso.com.mx. It also describes associated media properties/services: Agencia Proceso/APRO, Procesofoto, and ProcesoTV. MOM gives 1976 as the founding year and lists a CDMX contact address at Fresas 13, Colonia Del Valle, Benito Juarez.

Current leadership: multiple 2026/2025 sources identify Jorge Carrasco Araizaga as director of Proceso. Coverage of the 2019-2020 succession says he replaced Rafael Rodriguez Castaneda on February 1, 2020 after Rodriguez's retirement.

Ownership/person-level note: the outlet should be coded first by legal entity, `Comunicacion e Informacion, S.A. de C.V.`, not by one individual. Media Ownership Monitor says that after Julio Scherer Garcia's death, majority partners included Francisco Alvarez Romero, Estela Franco Arroyo, Salvador Corro Ortiz, Rafael Rodriguez Castaneda and Julio Scherer Ibarra. It also notes that Julio Scherer Ibarra later entered AMLO's government and resigned from Proceso's board in January 2019. Because ownership may have changed since MOM's 2018-era data, mark person-level ownership as needing verification if precision matters.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `investigative / watchdog / oppositional to incumbent power; historically anti-authoritarian`.

For the workbook's existing `opp` value, I would keep it only with a clarifying note: Proceso is not simply opposition-party aligned. Its historical brand is critical investigation of power, including PRI-era authoritarianism, PAN and PRI governments, and also the AMLO/4T government. The better metadata label is `watchdog` or `critical of incumbent power`.

Evidence:

- Media Ownership Monitor characterizes Proceso by a critical line against power and by investigations into corruption, forced disappearance, misuse of public resources and illicit enrichment.
- UNAM Global and Excelsior retrospectives connect the magazine's birth to the July 1976 conflict at Excelsior and the defense of press freedom against government pressure.
- A 2026 Vanguardia article quoting Jorge Carrasco says Proceso's founding vision was to contribute to building democracy in Mexico and to "subject facts and protagonists to process."
- The 2019-2020 transition coverage reports Carrasco saying the new direction would continue reviewing acts of power.
- AMLO himself publicly criticized Proceso in 2019, and Proceso staff/directors framed the exchange as an issue of executive intolerance toward critical press. That supports current-era critical/watchdog behavior, but not a party ideology.
