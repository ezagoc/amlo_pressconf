## Notes

El Siglo de Durango is a Durango-based regional newspaper linked to Grupo CELSA and Compania Editora de la Laguna, S.A. de C.V., the same broad media family as El Siglo de Torreon. Its own history/anniversary articles say it was founded on July 8, 1993, by Antonio de Juambelz y Bracho, growing out of a Durango section of El Siglo de Torreon that had operated since 1987.

The official directivos page lists Antonio de Juambelz as founder, Patricia Gonzalez-Karg de Juambelz in the presidency, Alejandro Irazoqui Morales as vice president of the board, and a directive committee including Patricia, Alfonso and Antonio Gonzalez-Karg de Juambelz. It lists Jorge Perez Arellano Romero as director. A 2020 article says Grupo CELSA's board ratified Jorge Perez Arellano Romero as Director General of El Siglo de Durango after he had worked his way through reporting/editing roles at the paper.

The official privacy notice identifies Compania Editora de la Laguna, S.A. de C.V. as the responsible legal company, with address at Calle Hidalgo 419 Sur, CP 34000, Durango, Durango. The SIC record gives the same address and lists the casa editorial as Compania Editora de la Laguna, S.A. de C.V.

Platform coding should be `print + digital + Siglo TV + social media`. The site navigation includes Siglo TV, the 2020 director appointment article mentions the growth of the digital page, social networks and Siglo TV/Siglovision, and the workbook already lists X/Twitter and YouTube.

## Ideology / Alignment Evidence

Recommended coding: `regional civic / independent by self-description; current national alignment unclear`.

Do not code as `aligned`, `opp`, pro-AMLO or anti-AMLO from the evidence currently found. The strongest direct source is the outlet's own editorial policy, inherited from the broader El Siglo doctrine, which states that the newspaper does not sell its criterion, rejects government favors/subsidies, and lives from advertising and circulation. This is a self-description, not external validation, but it supports an independent/regional-civic coding.

The AEE listing describes El Siglo de Durango as a serious/professional local and regional paper and an open forum of expression. That supports the regional/open-forum characterization, but not a partisan label. I did not find a strong academic or external source that clearly places the outlet in a current ideological camp.
