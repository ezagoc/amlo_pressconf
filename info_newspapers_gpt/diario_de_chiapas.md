## Notes

The official directory lists Gerardo Toledo Coutiño as president and director general and Rogelio Toledo Coutiño as general manager. A Chiapas government press directory gives the 4a. Oriente Sur address and phone numbers. Anniversary material from Diario de Chiapas states the contemporary edition was founded in 1975 and describes the newspaper/corporate group as part of the Toledo Coutiño family legacy, now operating as Diario Media Group with print, radio, TV/multimedia and social platforms.

I did not find a clean legal corporate name in this pass, so `legal_name` is left blank. Ideology is coded `center` / `unclear`; the sources document history and a critical/informational identity, but not a stable party or 4T alignment label.
