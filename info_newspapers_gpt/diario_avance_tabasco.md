## Notes
Diario Avance is part of the Sistema Informativo de Tabasco, together with Diario Presente and El Sol del Sureste. Its own 50-year note says Fernando Alcala Bates brought the Avance project to Villahermosa in 1971 and Ignacio Cobo Gonzalez later bought the title/logo in the 1980s. A 2025 anniversary note says Ignacio Cobo Gonzalez has owned it since 1981. I used the shared SIT/Presente address, but left legal name blank because I did not find a clean current imprint specific to Avance.
