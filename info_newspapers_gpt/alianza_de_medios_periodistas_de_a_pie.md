## Evidence summary

Alliance launched publicly in October 2018 under Red de Periodistas de a Pie, initially grouping 12 local outlets and later described as 15 independent media across multiple states.

## Ideology rationale

Its human-rights, feminist, social and community journalism commitments support a broad left label. Member outlets retain editorial autonomy, so no alliance-wide 4T position is assigned.

## Verification note

This is an alliance rather than a single outlet. The archive label's reference to Altavoz could not be reconciled with the official member lists, which have also changed over time.
