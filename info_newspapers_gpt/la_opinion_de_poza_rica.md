## Notes
MPM and SIC both list Editorial Gibb, S.A. de C.V. and the Mariano Arista address. MPM names Gonzalo Hernandez Gibb as director general and Norma Arango Gibb as administradora unica. The paper's own 70th anniversary note names Raul Gibb Quintero and Margarita Guerrero de Gibb as founders and refers to Silvia Gibb Guerrero and Jacob A. Buis Gibb in current leadership. Veracruz de los Silencios gives additional context about the Gibb family and internal disputes; treat current ownership with caution.
