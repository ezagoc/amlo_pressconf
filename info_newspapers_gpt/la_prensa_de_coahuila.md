## Notes

MPM lists La Prensa de Coahuila as a daily morning newspaper, founded November 13, 2005, with Monclova address and Mauricio Sánchez Campos as director general. Anniversary pieces identify Melchor Sánchez de la Fuente as president of the council and Mauricio Alfredo Sánchez Campos as director general. I did not find a strong official corporate legal name in this pass, so `legal_name` is blank.
