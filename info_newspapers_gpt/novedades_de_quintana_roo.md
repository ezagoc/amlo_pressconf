## Evidence and notes

Novedades de Quintana Roo is a Cancun-based daily newspaper tied to Grupo SIPSE. MPM lists `Novedades Quintana Roo` as a morning daily founded November 22, 1974, with the Calle Pecari No. 37 address in Cancun, and separately lists the outlet as a newspaper-internet product. SIC lists the publication as daily, gives the same Pecari/SM 20 address, identifies the casa editorial as `Novedades de Quintana Roo, S.A. de C.V.`, names Gerardo Garcia Gamboa as director, and lists AMME as an association. Grupo SIPSE's own contact page gives Novedades Quintana Roo's office at Pecari No. 37, SM 20, Mz. 6, Ret. 2, Cancun.

For ownership/control, the cleanest coding is Grupo SIPSE / Novedades-De Peso, with the Garcia Lavin / Garcia Gamboa family. Grupo SIPSE's own institutional page defines Grupo SIPSE as Servicios Informativos y Publicitarios del Sureste and names Andres Garcia Lavin as founder. SIC names Gerardo Garcia Gamboa as director of Novedades de Quintana Roo, while regional reporting/commentary describes Gerardo Garcia Gamboa as the figure heading the Novedades/print side in Quintana Roo. I would code Gerardo as a visible control/directorial figure rather than a proven sole owner.

For founding, use 1974. MPM gives November 22, 1974. A local anniversary note says Novedades de Quintana Roo first appeared in the state on November 22, 1974 and names Jose Concepcion `Pimpo` Pereyra Lizarraga as director fundador. Secondary biographical material on Andres Garcia Lavin says he founded Novedades de Quintana Roo in Cancun in 1974 as part of his media expansion.

For ideology, I would code `right / opp / low`. I did not find a formal declared ideology. The basis is mainly local political alignment evidence from 2022: Ruptura 360 and Caribe Peninsular report that the Novedades / Grupo SIPSE print-digital side supported Laura Fernandez, the PAN-PRD candidate, against Morena's Mara Lezama in the Quintana Roo gubernatorial race. Ruptura 360 also describes pro-Laura front-page/editorial behavior and says the paper tried to influence public opinion in her favor. This supports an anti-Morena / right-opposition coding, but confidence should remain low because it is based on electoral-cycle behavior and critical commentary rather than a formal editorial platform. There is also evidence of a later complicated relationship with Mara Lezama and local government advertising, so I would not overstate the label.
