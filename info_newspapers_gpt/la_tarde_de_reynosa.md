## Notes
La Tarde is the vespertino associated with Editora DEMAR/El Manana de Reynosa, not to be confused with El Dictamen-La Tarde in Veracruz. A 2016 printed directory scan says La Tarde was founded May 30, 1971, printed in Editora DEMAR's Matias Canales 504 workshops, and lists Orlando Deandar Ayala and Jorge J. Vargas Coello as directors. El Manana's directory supplies the current Deandar/Editora DEMAR context. I found no ideology evidence beyond the parent paper's general plural/freedom-of-expression language.
