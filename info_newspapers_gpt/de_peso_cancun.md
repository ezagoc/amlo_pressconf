## Evidence and notes

De Peso Cancun is best treated as the Cancun/Quintana Roo edition of the Grupo SIPSE popular-tabloid brand `De Peso`, rather than as a separate independent outlet. The De Peso brand is tied to SIPSE's Novedades newspaper operation in the peninsula. Search and directory evidence identifies `De Peso Quintana Roo` as based in Cancun, and active digital/social posts use `De Peso Quintana Roo` with the domain `depeso.com/quintana-roo/`.

For the address, I would use the Novedades/De Peso Cancun office at Calle Pecari No. 37, SM 20, Mz. 6, Ret. 2, Col. Centro, Cancun, C.P. 77500. Grupo SIPSE's own contact page lists Novedades Quintana Roo at that address. Noticaribe reports that Novedades de Quintana Roo and De Peso, both of Grupo SIPSE, shared that same Calle Pecari address. Older references also place Grupo SIPSE/Novedades/De Peso print operations in Cancun.

For legal name, I would leave the workbook field blank unless you want to use a broad umbrella entry. Grupo SIPSE is Servicios Informativos y Publicitarios del Sureste, and a Grupo SIPSE privacy page uses that corporate identity. However, I did not find a clean current `De Peso Cancun` imprint giving the exact publishing company. For that reason, `owner_group` is stronger than `legal_name`.

For ownership/control, code Grupo SIPSE / Novedades-De Peso. The SIPSE institutional page names Andres Garcia Lavin as founder of Grupo SIPSE. Public reporting and regional media commentary describe the print side in Quintana Roo as the Novedades-De Peso operation headed by Gerardo Garcia Gamboa, while distinguishing it from the television side associated with Alejandro Garcia Gamboa. I would use Gerardo Garcia Gamboa only as a control/visible-print-arm figure, not as a formally verified sole owner.

For founding year, use 2005 if the row is specifically `De Peso Cancun` or `De Peso Quintana Roo`; use 2004 if coding the general De Peso brand. De Peso Yucatan's own anniversary note dates the original De Peso brand to October 10, 2004 and refers to sister editions De Peso Quintana Roo, Riviera Maya, and Chetumal. A business-directory snippet gives the Quintana Roo start as April 20, 2005, but this should be treated as lower-grade evidence.

For ideology, I would code `right / opp / low`. The label is not based on the paper's declared ideology. De Peso is a popular/tabloid outlet focused on police, crime, sports, and entertainment; ideological content may be less direct than in a general political daily. The coding comes from the broader Novedades-De Peso / Grupo SIPSE Quintana Roo print arm: Ruptura 360 and Caribe Peninsular report that this side of Grupo SIPSE supported Laura Fernandez, the PAN-PRD candidate, against Morena's Mara Lezama in the 2022 Quintana Roo gubernatorial race. This supports a local anti-Morena / right-opposition tendency, but confidence should stay low because it is inferred from group-level electoral behavior and commentary rather than a formal editorial line.
