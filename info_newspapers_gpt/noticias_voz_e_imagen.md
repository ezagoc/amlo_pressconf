## Notes

Noticias Voz e Imagen is an Oaxaca-based newspaper and digital news outlet. Its own "Nosotros" page describes Grupo NVI as a media company operating through print, digital and video platforms. It says the first number of NOTICIAS Voz e Imagen de Oaxaca circulated on July 12, 1976, marking the public start of Editorial Taller, S.A. de C.V., under the general direction of Jose Martinez Bastida.

The current privacy policy identifies the service as Editorial Golfo Pacifico, S.A. de C.V. The Sistema de Informacion Cultural also lists the casa editorial as Editorial Golfo Pacifico, S.A. de C.V., with Ericel Gomez Nucamendi as director and Ivan Amancio Gomez Ramirez as jefe de redaccion. The SIC record lists daily periodicity and older certified print circulation figures for Jan.-Jun. 2009: 29,332 weekday/Saturday copies and 30,941 Sunday copies.

Ownership should not be coded as a confirmed person-level field from the current evidence. Several journalistic/commentary sources call Ericel Gomez Nucamendi the owner, and the Sistema de Informacion Legislativa profile describes him as founder/owner or president in related editorial companies. However, a 2006 TEPJF electoral ruling says a certified notarial act showed Gomez Nucamendi ceded his shareholder rights in Editorial Golfo Pacifico, S.A. de C.V. Because of that conflict, use `Editorial Golfo Pacifico, S.A. de C.V.` / `Grupo NVI` as the organization-level owner/legal entity and leave `owner_person` blank or `unverified`.

NVI's own historical articles give a useful chronology: creation/inauguration on July 1, 1976; first issue on July 12, 1976; Noticias Voz e Imagen de la Cuenca founded February 10, 1979; Noticias Voz e Imagen de Chiapas born August 6, 2007; Noticias Voz e Imagen del Istmo born June 14, 2013; web portal created in 1998 as noticiasnet.mx, now nvinoticias.com.

Political/ideological classification should be cautious. La Jornada described Noticias in 2006 as the only one of seven Oaxaca capital newspapers opposed to the Ulises Ruiz state government, during a period when the outlet suffered attacks/occupation and CIDH precautionary measures were requested. That supports a historical `opposition` or anti-PRI/Oaxaca-government label for the 2004-2006 period.

For the current era, evidence is less clean. Ericel Gomez Nucamendi has a political trajectory, including roles tied to Convergencia/Movimiento Ciudadano and later Morena according to secondary reporting. A 2025 Etcétera opinion column alleges that the outlet became pro-government/pro-4T and claimed the print edition had stopped circulating. Because this is commentary, and because person-level ownership is not firmly established, use it only as an `ideology.source` note rather than definitive coding. A good coding option is `historical opposition; current alignment unclear / possibly pro-4T`.
