## Evidence summary

National multimedia news brand of the Gonzalez family's Grupo Multimedios. The newspaper lineage begins with Diario de Monterrey in 1974; the national Milenio title launched in 2000. Coding is center/aligned because research finds source-dependent, government-amplifying coverage rather than a coherent left ideology.

## Ideology rationale

The workbook uses the deliberately simple labels shown above. A center/unclear/low entry means the available evidence did not justify a stronger partisan inference; it does not claim the outlet has no editorial tendencies.
