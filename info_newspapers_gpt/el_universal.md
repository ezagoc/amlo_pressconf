## Evidence summary

National daily founded on 1 October 1916 and controlled for decades by the Ealy family. Its 4T classification is opposition-oriented, while the broader left-right label remains center because the paper hosts diverse voices.

## Ideology rationale

The workbook uses the deliberately simple labels shown above. A center/unclear/low entry means the available evidence did not justify a stronger partisan inference; it does not claim the outlet has no editorial tendencies.

## Verification note

The left-right label is intrinsically mixed and should not be read as ideological neutrality. Current shareholding details beyond family control merit verification.
