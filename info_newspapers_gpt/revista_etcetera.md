## Evidence summary

Mexico City publication devoted to media criticism and national affairs. It began as a political and cultural weekly in 1993, entered a specialist media-analysis era in 2000 and ended regular print circulation in 2018.

## Ideology rationale

The outlet describes itself as institution-oriented and non-factional, which supports center. Its sustained books, covers and editorials portraying the 4T as deceptive or authoritarian support opposition coding with high confidence.
