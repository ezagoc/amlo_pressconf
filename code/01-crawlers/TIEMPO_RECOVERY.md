Author: Kevin. This release prepares one explicit trial. It does not resume the
collector, replace the current pointer, clear a halt, approve articles, or mark
Tiempo complete.

## Entry points

`python scripts/run_tiempo_recovery.py prepare --config <config.json>` is offline
and refuses an existing destination. `inspect --capsule <capsule-directory>`
checks the exact frozen program, original evidence, native prefix and budget.
Only `trial --capsule <capsule-directory> --allow-network` enables live transport.
An explicit subsequent user authorization is required before invoking that
command for the real capsule. This release never invokes it automatically.

The real October 7 bridge is limited to the last 14 URLs of original child 23,
batch 2 (66 already saved). There are at most 42 new physical transport calls,
including retries; the old 44,184 calls remain charged. Even a repeated completed
invocation makes zero requests. It cannot advance to batch 3 or another child.
Expansion after this boundary requires a reviewed successor plan, not a deleted
halt or a silently raised budget. The unchanged 86,385-row remaining-scope ledger
continues to describe all unfinished work, not just the trial.

## Historical identity and source risks

A frozen, source-bound identity index carries canonical/body relationships
across restarts and children. Repeated records remain separate. Duplicate
relationships are research judgments, recorded with previous sample IDs; they
are not blanket prerequisites for downloading unrelated articles. Wrong source
identity, actual lost prose, true publication-date conflicts, damaged evidence,
service/access failures and disk/budget problems retain their technical stops.
The title/date/video/social parsing rules from Priority 2 are unchanged.

## Recovery and rollback

A complete cached response can finish its native save without another download.
Unknown transport windows, changed hashes or incomplete retry chains stop for
investigation. Never remove a halt or its permanent receipt to continue.
Before running a trial, preserve the prepared capsule and use its exact bound
worktree, configuration and input files. If a trial stops, retain its outputs
and logs, identify the cause, and prepare a new bounded version after review.
Rollback means abandoning that new trial version and continuing to retain the
original paused donor and pointer; it does not mean restarting the donor.

The prepared runtime is private and isolated. Its archive and readiness report
are shared in Dropbox. Trial outputs must be reviewed and then copied into a
new English-named Kevin directory in the shared crawler structure; never merge
synthetic rehearsal data into news outputs or the fixed 2,670-row delivery.
